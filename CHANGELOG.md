# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- New skill `hubpost-clipper`: turns a livestream (live or VOD), a podcast or any long video into finished vertical clips on your own computer, then offers `hubpost-publish` to schedule them.
- `hubpost-publish` chains after `hubpost-clipper`.
- CI checks every skill's scripts as well as its frontmatter.

## 0.1.0

- First release with `hubpost-publish`.
